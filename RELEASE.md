# Release v1.0.0

**Repository:** `ITMOSOFT/laravel-shipping`  
**Description:** Shipping zones, methods, rates, quotes, shipments and tracking for Laravel applications.

## Highlights
- Configurable shipping methods and destination zones.
- Country, region and postal-code zone matching.
- Flat, weight-based and order-subtotal rate calculation.
- Quote service and shipment lifecycle with tracking events.
- Configurable table prefix and publishable configuration/migrations.

## Requirements
- PHP 8.3+
- Laravel 12 or 13

## Scope exclusions
No courier API, live carrier rates, label purchasing, customs, tax, payment, or automatic order status synchronization.
